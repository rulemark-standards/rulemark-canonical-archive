# RM-S-STABLE-001 Canonical Summary

Defines deterministic, machine-verifiable requirements for the trust basis of fiat-referenced, reserve-backed stablecoins, covering supply, liabilities, reserves, custody, redemption, and independent attestation. It determines whether a stablecoin satisfies those requirements at a specified verification point. It does not predict market price, does not guarantee against de-pegging, and does not certify legal compliance in any jurisdiction.

The frozen PDF is the sole Normative Artifact. Supporting Markdown, schema, verifier, and machine records aid implementation and discovery but do not override the PDF.
