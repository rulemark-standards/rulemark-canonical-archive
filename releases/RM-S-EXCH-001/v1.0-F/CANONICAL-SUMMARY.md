# RM-S-EXCH-001 Canonical Summary

Defines deterministic, machine-verifiable requirements for the transparency of custodial digital asset trading platforms, covering the complete set of entities holding customer assets, cryptographic proof of control over reserve addresses, customer-verifiable liabilities, and per-asset reserve coverage. It determines whether a platform satisfies those requirements at a single specified verification point, and does not describe a continuing state. It does not guarantee future solvency, going concern, or the safety of customer assets, does not assess listed assets or market conduct, and does not certify the validity of any licence.

The frozen PDF is the sole Normative Artifact. Supporting Markdown, schema, verifier, and machine records aid implementation and discovery but do not override the PDF.
