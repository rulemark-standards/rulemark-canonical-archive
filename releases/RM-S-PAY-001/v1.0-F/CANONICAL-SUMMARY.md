# RM-S-PAY-001 Canonical Summary

Defines deterministic, machine-verifiable requirements for the authenticity, completeness, and re-computability of digital payment records, from authorization through settlement and finality.

RM-S-PAY-001 determines whether a digital payment record is authentic, complete, internally consistent, and re-computable at a specified verification time. It does not certify the lawfulness of the source of funds, KYC, AML, sanctions, or tax compliance, and does not adjudicate dispute liability.

The frozen PDF is the sole Normative Artifact. Supporting Markdown, schema, verifier, and machine records aid implementation and discovery but do not override the PDF.
